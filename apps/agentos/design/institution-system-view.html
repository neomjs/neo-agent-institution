Tokens consumed, nothing minted

  • Type — the five §04 roles only: display for the card names and lane words, body for the diagnosis line, detail for facts, micro for ids, chips and times, chrome for eyebrows.
  • Color — ink tiers for text; the card's left mark rebinds one custom property per state, the chip idiom. Every card keeps its mark in the same place.
  • Rhythm — space-1…4 for every gap; the card's 16px left pad clears the 3px mark, the roster card's recorded exception, reused.
  • Elevation — none: in-flow panels stay border-carried; the lanes use panel-2 to sit one step back from the cards.

Scope, by surface

  • This view (the wire today): per-service status · memoryPressure · restartChurn · classification · diagnosis; the backup lane's retry phase, health verdict and last receipt; starvation as posture + breachCount — enough for the cards and the three lanes.
  • The scheduler view (its own surface, Tasks-pane lineage): the deferred-task queue with waiting-since and starved-for, the lease holder and its status, the backup lane's attempts — see the Tasks-queue sketch (neomjs/neo-agent-institution#113).
  • The roster (not here): seat hooks and presence beacons; per-service logs — the Brain's own verb.

Deliberately absent

  • Verbs. No restart, no drain, no "fix": the actuator class stays off this surface.
  • Per-card guessing. An unavailable or unsupported wire is one reason for the whole view; cards never invent a plane.
  • A fabricated log stream. The logs region names its sibling and stays empty until the verb exists.
  • Host paths, config, container ids beyond the compose id. Redacted at the wire; the card shows the name, the compose id, and what the orchestrator concluded.

Open for review
