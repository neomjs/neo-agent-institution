The default perspective gives each important view a good home

The operator, 2026-10-03: dock layouts, splitters, tear-out and popups make views adjustable; that is no excuse for a default structure that is not good. This page decides the one default — the Overview duty in apps/agentos/util/CockpitPerspectives.mjs — at the operator's own window. The measure is his capture of 2026-10-09 02:58Z, the first morning all eight active peers ran from the Fleet Manager, as the 2000 × 1217 px copy pasted into this seat's session (a resize of the original; the proportions survive, aspect 1.64). The window's logical size and display scale are not read by this page: every measure below is a capture px of that copy, and no logical figure is asserted (§06). Both frames are drawn to the capture's proportions, not screenshotted.

Three moves. (1) The dock's center becomes a horizontal split, 0.64 : 0.36: the fleet-over-feeds stack on the left, a full-height reading column on the right. (2) The six-tab strip splits by nature: the two time-ordered feeds (Activity, Catch up) stay under the roster; the four surfaces one reads or acts on (Agent Detail, Memories, Mailbox, Tasks) and the Golden Path move into the column, Memories open at first run. Detail shares the column with the reading views: selecting a peer while Memories or Mailbox is the open tab takes the column to Detail, and the reading tab is one click back, where today's committed band leaves the strip untouched beside it. #632, a retained pane receiving the wrong record during that transition, is a defect of the transition, not of placement, and stays separate from this page.

A tabs node carries activeItemId (WorkspaceDocument.mjs, the tabs field set; an absent one is repaired to the first item) — so Agent Detail can lead the strip while Memories opens. The inspector docked as a center member keeps its catalog autoHidden flag and stays in the tab flow, as Review already relies on; revealsInspector reads placement, not the flag. Selection today reveals the rail member (Controller#applySelection); with the column it activates the tab instead — one dock operation, the builder's line. Focus keeps its roster-dominant split. Review keeps its duty with the same column widened to 0.5 and Detail active — a one-number change the builder makes beside this one. Saved captures key the old node ids and restore as they were; Reset to default restores this tree (AC-3).

06 · what this page does not verify

· The window's logical size and display scale are unread: the page measures a 2000 × 1217 resized copy of the capture, so every px here is a capture px and no logical figure is asserted. The builder's NL arm reads the installed viewport (get_dom_rect) before the split numbers are final; the card width at two columns follows that read.
· Two-column cards at ≈ 586 capture px are computed, not rendered; CARD-CONTRACT.md's two-row anatomy is assumed to hold within 6 % of today's width — the unit arm on the catalog and the visual run (AC-4) falsify it.
· Memories open at first run assumes the plane holds summaries; an empty plane shows the pane's honest empty state, never sample data.
· The 0.64 : 0.36 split is a proposal for the operator's approval (AC-1, Tier 4 — the default is his product opinion); the page records the numbers so the approval is of something measured.
